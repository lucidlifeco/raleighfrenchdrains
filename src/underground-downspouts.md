# Underground Downspout Drains in Raleigh

Your roof collects a surprising amount of water, and the downspouts drop all of it in a few spots right next to the house. If those spots stay soggy, wash out mulch, or sit beside a damp crawl space, the fix is usually to carry that water away underground.

## How much water we're talking about

An inch of rain on one acre is about 27,154 gallons.<sup><a href="#s1">1</a></sup> Scaled to a 2,000-square-foot roof, that's roughly **1,250 gallons per inch of rain**, split among just a few downspouts.

Raleigh's wettest month, September, averages about 5.1 inches of rain.<sup><a href="#s2">2</a></sup> For that same roof, that's roughly 6,000 gallons in a typical September, all landing beside your foundation unless something moves it.

## How underground downspout lines work

A solid pipe connects to the bottom of each downspout, runs underground away from the house, and ends at an outlet:

- **Pop-up emitter:** a cap in the lawn that pops open when water flows and closes when it stops
- **Daylight outlet:** the pipe opens on lower ground, a ditch, or the curb, where allowed
- **[Dry well](/dry-wells/):** a buried pit that holds the water and lets it soak in
- **Tie-in to a [French drain](/french-drain-installation/) or [catch basin](/catch-basins/) system**

Downspout lines should use solid pipe, not perforated. You want roof water carried away, not leaking back into the soil next to the house.

## What it costs

Underground downspout drains typically cost **$200 to $2,000 per downspout**.<sup><a href="#s3">3</a></sup> The low end is a short run to a pop-up emitter. The high end is a long run around the house to the street or a dry well.

## Signs your downspouts are the problem

- Mulch or soil washed out below the downspout after storms
- A soggy strip along the side of the house
- Water stains or dampness in the crawl space near a downspout
- Splash-back dirt on siding or the foundation

## Before you bury anything

- **Clean the gutters first.** Clogged gutters overflow and dump water everywhere, no matter where the downspouts go.
- **Plan the outlet carefully.** Water sent to a neighbor's yard can create liability under North Carolina's reasonable use rule.<sup><a href="#s4">4</a></sup>
- **Call 811** at least three full working days before digging.<sup><a href="#s5">5</a></sup>
