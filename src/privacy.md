# Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026.

**Who runs this site.** {{ site.name }} is operated by Cody Martin.

**What we collect.** When you call, we receive your phone number and a recording and transcript of the call. When you send a request through a form, we receive what you enter, such as your name, phone number, location and a description of your project. Our hosting provider may also log basic technical information like your IP address and browser type.

**How we use it.** We use your information to connect you with one independent local drainage contractor so they can contact you about your project, and to confirm that calls were answered and handled. Recordings and transcripts may be shared with that contractor.

**Who we share it with.** The contractor we connect you with, and the service providers we use to run this site and route calls. We don't sell your information to data brokers.

**Text messages.** If we miss your call, we may send one text message to the number you called from so you can reply about your project. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

**How long we keep it.** We keep call records and requests only as long as we need them for the purposes above.

**Your choices.** To ask what we have about you or to have it deleted, email {{ site.contactEmail }}.

**Changes.** We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
