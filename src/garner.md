# French Drains and Yard Drainage in Garner

Water standing in the backyard after a storm, a wet crawl space, or runoff cutting through the lawn: Garner homeowners deal with all of it. Talk to a local drainage pro who works in Garner: French drains, regrading, catch basins, downspout lines and dry wells. One call gets you started.

## Start with a free consult from the Town

Garner makes this easy. If you have a drainage or erosion issue on your property, the Town asks you to email **stormwater@garnernc.gov** to schedule a stormwater consult. Its storm drain policy lays out what the Town is responsible for maintaining on public and private property.<sup><a href="#s1">1</a></sup>

That consult tells you whether the problem involves Town infrastructure. If it's your yard, your grading or your downspouts, the fix is usually yours to make, and that's where a local drainage pro comes in.

## Why runoff matters in Garner

Most of Garner drains to **Lake Benson**, a drinking water supply for City of Raleigh customers. The Town holds two state stormwater permits and sits within a water supply watershed protection area.<sup><a href="#s1">1</a></sup> Some neighborhoods also have stormwater control measures, like ponds, that the property owner or HOA must inspect every year.<sup><a href="#s2">2</a></sup>

For you, that means drainage work should control mud and erosion on your lot while it's underway, and send water somewhere sensible when it's done.

## When the water comes from next door

Wake County's guidance sums up North Carolina drainage law: the lower property must receive and pass water that naturally flows from higher ground, and you may reroute surface runoff to or near the main low point of your own property.<sup><a href="#s3">3</a></sup> Easement and runoff disputes between neighbors are generally private civil matters.<sup><a href="#s3">3</a></sup>

The fixes that work with that rule:

- A **[swale](/yard-grading-swales/)** that steers water around the house toward your low point
- A **[French drain](/french-drain-installation/)** to catch water soaking through from uphill
- **[Underground downspout lines](/underground-downspouts/)** to get roof water away from the foundation

## What drainage work costs

- **Yard French drains** in the Raleigh area typically cost **$20 to $50 per linear foot**, or about $1,970 to $6,880 for a typical project.<sup><a href="#s4">4</a></sup>
- See the [French drain cost guide](/french-drain-cost-raleigh/) for prices by length and how other fixes compare.

## Before anyone digs

- **Call 811** at least three full working days before digging so utility lines get marked.<sup><a href="#s5">5</a></sup>
- **Check your plat for drainage easements.** On a shared easement, each owner is typically responsible for the part on their own lot.<sup><a href="#s3">3</a></sup>
- **Ask where the water will end up.** A good drainage pro will show you the outlet before work starts.
