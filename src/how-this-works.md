## Good to know

**The pros do the work.** {{ site.name }} connects homeowners with local drainage pros. Each pro runs their own business and sets their own prices, schedules and warranties. As with any home project, ask for proof of insurance and any license that applies, and get the price and scope in writing.

**It's free for homeowners.** We may be paid by the pros we refer.

**Calls may be recorded** to make sure every caller gets answered and helped.

**Prices on this site are typical ranges** from published cost guides, with sources listed on each page. They're meant to help you budget, not to quote your job.
