# Catch Basin Installation in Raleigh

If water collects in the same low spot every time it rains, such as the corner of the yard, the bottom of the driveway, or beside the patio, a catch basin may be the fix.

## What a catch basin is

A catch basin is a box set into the ground at the low spot, with a grate on top. Water flows in through the grate, any debris settles to the bottom, and a solid pipe carries the water to an outlet: the street, a ditch, a [dry well](/dry-wells/), or lower ground.

It's a collection point, not a whole system. Most catch basins are paired with buried pipe, and often with a [French drain](/french-drain-installation/) or [underground downspout lines](/underground-downspouts/).

## Good places for a catch basin

- The lowest spot in the yard, where puddles form first
- The bottom of a driveway or walkway that slopes toward the house
- Next to a patio or pool deck that sheds water onto the lawn
- Where a downspout empties and the ground can't take the water

## What it costs in Raleigh

A catch basin installation typically runs **$590 to $1,970** in Raleigh.<sup><a href="#s1">1</a></sup> The price depends mainly on how far the pipe has to run to reach an outlet. A basin next to the street is cheap. One in the back corner of a deep lot isn't.

For a complete drainage plan, see the [Raleigh French drain cost guide](/french-drain-cost-raleigh/), which compares catch basins with French drains, swales, and dry wells.

## Keeping it working

- Clear leaves and mulch off the grate, especially in fall.
- Lift the grate a couple of times a year and scoop out the sediment at the bottom.
- Check the outlet after big storms to make sure water is actually leaving.

## Before installation

Call 811 at least three full working days before digging so utility lines are marked.<sup><a href="#s2">2</a></sup> And plan the outlet so the water doesn't end up on a neighbor's property. North Carolina's reasonable use rule can make you liable if it causes unreasonable harm.<sup><a href="#s3">3</a></sup>
