# French Drain Installation in Raleigh

A French drain is a trench lined with filter fabric, filled with gravel, and laid with a perforated pipe. Water in the soggy ground seeps into the gravel, enters the pipe, and flows downhill to an outlet away from your house and the problem area.

It's the most common fix for a Raleigh yard that stays wet after rain, and one of the most misunderstood. Here's what to know before you get quotes.

## When a French drain is the right fix

- **Ground that stays soggy for days** after a storm, even when the surface looks dry
- **Water seeping toward the foundation** or into a crawl space from saturated soil
- **A wet strip at the bottom of a slope**, where water from uphill soaks through
- **Low areas with no way to regrade**, because a fence, a patio, or the property line is in the way

## When something else works better

- **Water running across the surface in sheets.** A [swale or regrading](/yard-grading-swales/) usually handles this for less.
- **Water pooling at one low spot.** A [catch basin](/catch-basins/) collects it and pipes it away.
- **Water dumping out of downspouts.** [Underground downspout lines](/underground-downspouts/) fix the source.
- **No low spot to drain to.** A [dry well](/dry-wells/) can hold the water and let it soak in.

Many yards need a combination. A good drainage pro walks the property in the rain, or right after it, before recommending anything.

## How installation works

1. **Find where the water comes from and where it can go.** Every French drain needs a lower outlet: the street, a ditch, a dry well, or a pop-up emitter in the lawn.
2. **Call 811.** North Carolina law requires notice at least three full working days before digging so buried utilities get marked.<sup><a href="#s1">1</a></sup>
3. **Dig the trench with a steady downhill slope** from the wet area to the outlet. Exterior French drains are usually about 2 to 3 feet deep.<sup><a href="#s2">2</a></sup>
4. **Line the trench with filter fabric, add gravel, and lay perforated pipe.** The fabric keeps clay and silt from clogging the gravel.
5. **Backfill and restore the surface** with gravel, topsoil and sod, or mulch.

## What it costs in Raleigh

Yard French drains in Raleigh typically run **$20 to $50 per linear foot**, or about **$1,970 to $6,880** for a typical project.<sup><a href="#s3">3</a></sup> See the full [Raleigh French drain cost guide](/french-drain-cost-raleigh/) for prices by length and by type of drainage fix.

## Questions to ask before you hire

- Where exactly will the water go, and is that outlet allowed?
- How deep will the trench be, and what's the slope?
- What pipe, gravel and fabric are you using?
- Will you install a cleanout so the pipe can be flushed later?
- Is lawn or landscape repair included?
- What does your warranty cover, and for how long?

## Keeping it working

Shallow French drain pipes can usually be cleaned by flushing them with a hose.<sup><a href="#s2">2</a></sup> A cleanout port at the high end makes that easy. Keep the outlet clear of leaves and mulch, and check it after big storms.
