## What a French drain costs in Cary

- **Per foot:** French drains in Cary run about **$23 to $36 per linear foot**.<sup><a href="#s1">1</a></sup>
- **By length:** about $460 to $720 for 20 feet, $920 to $1,430 for 40 feet, and $1,380 to $2,150 for 60 feet.<sup><a href="#s1">1</a></sup>

## Who's responsible for drainage in Cary

**The Town maintains its own system, and you maintain yours.** Cary maintains stormwater infrastructure in the Town right-of-way and on Town-owned property. Storm drainage infrastructure outside the right-of-way is the property owner's responsibility, as noted on the "Certificate of Ownership and Dedication" required on subdivision plats recorded in Cary.<sup><a href="#s4">4</a></sup>

**Drainage easements matter.** Easements on recorded plats give upstream owners the right to drain stormwater runoff across downstream properties.<sup><a href="#s4">4</a></sup> If a drainage easement runs through your yard, check it before planning any work there.

**Ponds belong to the HOA.** Stormwater ponds in Cary neighborhoods are usually built as stormwater control measures, and the homeowners association maintains them.<sup><a href="#s5">5</a></sup>

## Changing drainage on your property

According to Cary's drainage FAQ, you can make changes to drainage on your own property, but you're responsible for making sure they don't cause substantial damage to others' property rights. Cary also notes that blocking the natural flow of water is illegal under North Carolina's drainage statutes.<sup><a href="#s5">5</a></sup>

In practice:

- A French drain or swale should carry water toward where it naturally flows, not dam it up or dump it on a neighbor.
- If you're working in or near a drainage easement, talk with the Town first.
- Cary offers technical guidance to residents on drainage improvements and green stormwater techniques on private property.<sup><a href="#s6">6</a></sup>
