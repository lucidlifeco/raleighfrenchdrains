# Standing Water in Your Yard: Causes and Fixes

A few puddles right after a storm are normal. Water that's still there a day or two later isn't. It kills grass, softens the ground under patios and walkways, and gives mosquitoes a place to breed.

## How long is too long?

Water that stands for five days or more can become a mosquito breeding site.<sup><a href="#s1">1</a></sup> According to the CDC, a mosquito egg takes about 7 to 10 days to develop into an adult.<sup><a href="#s2">2</a></sup> If your low spots routinely hold water for days after rain, it's worth fixing.

## How much water a yard handles

One inch of rain on a half-acre lot is about 13,577 gallons of water.<sup><a href="#s3">3</a></sup> Most of it should soak in or run off. When it collects instead, there's usually a clear cause.

## Why it happens so often around Raleigh

Much of the area sits on red clay. Cecil soil, North Carolina's state soil, has a firm, sticky clay subsoil.<sup><a href="#s4">4</a></sup> When construction strips topsoil and packs that clay down, rain soaks in slowly and low spots hold water.

## Is the town responsible?

Usually not. Raleigh Stormwater maintains the stormwater system on public streets and property, and homeowners are responsible for stormwater pipes and streams on their own property.<sup><a href="#s5">5</a></sup> Cary and Apex work the same way. See our [Cary](/cary/) and [Apex](/apex/) pages for the details there.

If the water comes from a public street or a clogged public storm drain, report it to your town. If it comes from a neighbor's property, North Carolina's reasonable use rule applies: owners can change how water drains, but can be liable for unreasonable harm to others.<sup><a href="#s6">6</a></sup>

## What to do next

1. **Watch the next storm.** Note where water comes from, where it collects, and how long it stays. Photos help.
2. **Check the easy things first.** Clean gutters, and extend downspouts away from the house.
3. **Get the yard looked at.** A drainage pro can tell you which fix, or combination, your yard needs and what it will cost. See typical prices in our [Raleigh French drain cost guide](/french-drain-cost-raleigh/).
