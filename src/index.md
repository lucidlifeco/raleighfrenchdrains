## Signs your yard needs drainage work

- Puddles that are still there a day after the rain stops
- Water pooling against the foundation, under the deck, or by the patio
- Mushy lawn, moss, or bare spots in low areas
- Mulch or soil washing across walkways and driveways
- Downspouts dumping water right next to the house
- A damp crawl space or a musty smell under the house

## Common fixes

### [French drain](/french-drain-installation/)

A gravel-filled trench with a perforated pipe that collects water underground and carries it to a lower spot. The go-to fix for a yard that stays wet.

{% figure "french-drain-cross-section.svg", "Cross-section of a French drain: a fabric-lined gravel trench with a perforated pipe at the bottom", "A French drain collects water from soggy soil and carries it to a lower outlet." %}

### [Swale or regrading](/yard-grading-swales/)

Reshaping the ground so water flows away from the house instead of toward it. Often the cheapest fix when the slope is the problem.

{% figure "grading-and-swale.svg", "Ground sloping at least 6 inches away from a house in the first 10 feet, with a grassy swale beyond", "Ground should drop at least 6 inches in the first 10 feet from the house." %}

### [Catch basin](/catch-basins/)

A grated box set at the low spot where water collects, piped to an outlet.

### [Underground downspouts](/underground-downspouts/)

Pipes that carry roof water away from the foundation instead of dumping it beside it.

### [Dry well](/dry-wells/)

A buried pit that holds runoff and lets it soak into the ground slowly, for yards with no good outlet.

Not sure which one you need? A quick call with a local pro will point you to the right fix, or the right combination, for your yard.

## Why Raleigh yards hold water

Much of the Raleigh area sits on red clay. Cecil soil, North Carolina's state soil, has a red clay subsoil that's firm and sticky ([USDA](https://soilseries.sc.egov.usda.gov/OSD_Docs/C/Cecil.html)). When building a house strips the topsoil and packs that clay down, rain has a harder time soaking in. Add steady rain all year, with September the wettest month at about 5.1 inches on average ([NOAA data](https://www.extremeweatherwatch.com/cities/raleigh/average-rainfall-by-month)), and low spots stay wet.
